Welcome to the British Railways Steam Locomotive Speed Calculator!

This tool was born out of a fun challenge back in 2023, while developing a heritage railway game on Rec Room (RIP). To make the wheel animations look completely realistic, I needed a reliable way to match wheel rotation to train speed. That sparked a simple, once-niche formula that works just as brilliantly for estimating real-world steam locomotive speeds on the tracks today as it does in simulations.

Rather than keeping the technique under wraps, I’ve shared it here so fellow enthusiasts, modellers, and developers can easily calculate a locomotive’s true speed in just a few quick steps.

How to Calculate Locomotive Speed & Wheel RPM

Field Guide & Engineering Principles

How to Find the RPM Visually

Imagine (or pull up a lineside video on your phone or computer) a steam locomotive travelling along at speed. Keep your eye fixed firmly on the big end—the large brass-bronzework bearing that links the main connecting rod to the driving wheel's crankpin.

Picture a beep sounding or a tap registered each time that big end swings around and passes its bottom-most point (bottom dead centre). Tapping along with that motion into a BPM counter yields your exact Wheel RPM (Revolutions Per Minute).

You can do this directly below using the Tap 1 Full Wheel Rev button or the [R] key.

How to Find the RPM by Sound

Firstly, identify the locomotive class to check its cylinder layout. For instance, an LMS Stanier Black Five has 2 cylinders, an LNER A4 Pacific has 3, and a GWR Castle has 4.

Listen closely to the exhaust beats. Each cylinder vents steam twice per revolution (once forward, once backward), creating the engine's rhythmic chuff:

  • 2 or 4-Cylinder Locomotives: Produce 4 chuffs per wheel revolution. You can tap every single chuff with the Tap Exhaust Beat button, or tap only on every 4th beat using the Wheel RPM button.
  • 3-Cylinder Locomotives: With 120° crank separation, they produce 6 chuffs per wheel revolution. Tap each individual chuff and select 6 Chuffs / Rev, or tap once on every 6th beat.
